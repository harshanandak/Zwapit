## Provider pricing (verified 2026-08-30)
- **Zoho ZeptoMail**: INR 150 + tax per 10k, 6-month credit, no monthly, first 10k free 1 month. Verified on rendered https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/pricing.html (rung 5). REST https://api.zeptomail.com/v1.1/email with API key (https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/api/email-sending.html). Webhooks for bounces/opens/clicks (https://www.zoho.com/zeptomail/help/webhooks.html). Pay-as-you-go, credits valid 6 months.
- **Resend**: 100/day, 3k/month free, then $20/month (https://resend.com/pricing.md). Simpler but caps low volume.
- **Cloudflare Email Sending**: 3k/month included on Workers Paid, then $0.35/1k, outbound still Beta, daily quota unspecified (https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/pricing/, /platform/limits/).
- Decision: ZeptoMail wins low-volume cost + adequate REST + observability. Evidence rung 2 for capabilities, rung 5 for price.

## Watcher contracts (origin/master)
- Collapse key: `catalogItemId|city|date|format` including empty trailing format (convex/watcher/parse.ts:computeCollapseKey). Theatre/venue NOT part of collapse; theatresJson is payload only.
- monitor_targets collapseKey index by_collapse_key, notification_queue idempotent on dedupeKey userId|monitorTargetId|availabilityEventId|alertType|channel.
- Senders: defaultSenders.email and webpush are env-gated no-ops pending recipient/subscription wiring (convex/watcher/senders.ts:101,118,142). Adding ZeptoMail replaces email sender; webpush stays stubbed.
- Channels validator: email | web_push only (convex/schema.ts:161); wants.channels optional array.
- Audit: every watcher mutation writes audit_logs; createAlert is one of two documented client-facing exceptions (AGENTS.md:18).

## Catalog & request fields
- wants: catalogItemId, quantity, maxPricePerUnit, watchCity/watchDate/watchFormat, alertTypes, channels, monitorTargetId, collapseKey (convex/schema.ts:406).
- Price NOT a Search filter; maxPrice only caps community resale. Theatre selection is client filter, not watcher shard.
- Community matching FIFO by creation time within quantity/price fit; allocation unaffected by tier.
